Privacy Policy

Tower Climb

Data Collection

  • No personal data is collected — We do not collect names, emails, or any personally identifiable information
  • No account required — The game can be played without signing up or logging in
  • No analytics tracking — We do not track your gameplay behavior or device information
  • No internet connection required — The game runs fully offline and does not send data over the network

Local Storage

  • Game settings (such as your volume level) are stored locally on your device
  • This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server
  • You can clear this data by uninstalling the app or clearing app data

Third-Party Services

  • Currently, no third-party services are integrated
  • If future updates add services such as ads (Google AdMob), this privacy policy will be updated to disclose them

Children's Privacy

This game is suitable for all ages.

We do not knowingly collect any data from children, or from anyone else.
