Core Features

One tower, no end in sight

🗼

Endless Climb

Procedurally generated platforms mean no two runs are the same. The tower just keeps going.

👆

One-Thumb Controls

Tap the left half of the screen to move left, the right half to move right. That's it.

🚩

Checkpoint Floors

Every 100 floors the camera stops on a checkpoint so you can catch your breath before the next stretch.

🌿

Six Biomes

Climb from mossy stone through ancient ruins, desert, snow, and volcanic rock, each with its own music.

🔥

Combos

Chain clean landings to build your combo. The HUD tracks your floor count and streak as you climb.

👾

Glitch Mode

Make it past the final stage and the tower starts to break apart, flickering between every biome.

How to Play

Easy to pick up, hard to put down

1

Tap to Launch

Start on the ground floor and tap anywhere to begin your run.

2

Steer Your Bounce

You bounce automatically on every platform you land on. Tap left or right to steer onto the next one.

3

Keep Ahead of the Camera

The camera scrolls upward on its own and speeds up as you climb. Fall below the screen and your run ends.

4

Reach a Checkpoint

Every 100 floors the camera settles on a checkpoint. Tap again to resume when you're ready.

5

Climb Higher

Push through each biome and see how many floors you can conquer before the tower wins.
