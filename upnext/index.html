Core Features

Local reuse made simple, trusted, and location-aware

📦

Listings Management

Create free item listings with photos, categories, and map-based locations. Edit or delete your active posts anytime.

📍

Location Discovery

Browse nearby listings with adjustable radius filters and category search to find items close to you.

📅

Booking Workflow

Reserve available items and track listing status from active to booked with automatic state updates.

💬

In-App Chat

Coordinate pickups through one-to-one messaging with realtime updates between givers and receivers.

🔐

Auth & Verification

Secure sign-up with Supabase Auth, email verification, and protected routes for a trusted community experience.

⚡

Realtime Feed

Live listing updates powered by Supabase realtime — new items and status changes appear instantly.

How It Works

From clutter to community reuse in four steps

1

List Items

Post unwanted but useful items with photos, a description, and your location on the map.

2

Discover Nearby

Browse the home feed, filter by category, and find free items within your chosen radius.

3

Book & Connect

Reserve an item and chat with the owner to arrange a convenient pickup time.

4

Give Items New Life

Reduce landfill waste, free up storage space, and help your community reuse what still matters.
