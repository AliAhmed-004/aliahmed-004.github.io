Privacy Policy

Sand Fall

Data Collection

  • No personal data is collected — We do not collect names, emails, or any other information that identifies you directly
  • No account required — The game can be played without signing up or logging in
  • Anonymous usage analytics — We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played so we can improve it. Details are under "Third-Party Services" below

Local Storage

  • High scores and game saves are stored locally on your device using Hive
  • Game saves never leave your device. If you sign in to Google Play Games, your high score is submitted to the leaderboard by Google Play Games
  • You can clear local data by uninstalling the app or clearing app data

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Analytics (Google) collects anonymous usage and device data: game interactions (mode, score, play time), device model, Android version, app version, approximate country, and an app-instance identifier that may include your device's advertising ID. It is not linked to your name or email
  • Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase's privacy information
  • You can reset this data by clearing app data or uninstalling the app, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings
  • Google Play Games is used for leaderboards if you choose to sign in
  • Future updates may include ads (Google AdMob), which will be disclosed in an updated privacy policy

Children's Privacy

This game is intended for ages 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions or concerns, feel free to reach out via email.
