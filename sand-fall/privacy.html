Privacy Policy

Sand Fall

Data Collection

  • No account required — You can play Sand Fall without signing up or logging in.
  • No direct personal information collection — We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying information.
  • Third-party data collection — We use Google Firebase Analytics and Google AdMob. These services may collect device information, usage data, identifiers, and other technical information as described below.

Local Storage

  • High scores and game saves are stored locally on your device using Hive.
  • Game saves remain on your device. If you sign in to Google Play Games, your high score is submitted to Google Play Games for leaderboard functionality.
  • You can remove locally stored game data by uninstalling the app or clearing its data through your Android settings.

Third-Party Services

Firebase Analytics (Google)

  • We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players interact with the game and improve the experience.
  • Information may include game interactions (game mode, score, play time), device model, Android version, app version, approximate country, and an app-instance identifier.
  • This information is used for analytics and understanding game usage.

Google AdMob

  • We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within Sand Fall.
  • AdMob may automatically collect and process information such as IP address, device identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID where available), app interactions, and diagnostic information.
  • This information may be used for delivering advertisements, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud, and improving advertising services.
  • Depending on your device settings, applicable consent requirements, and advertising configuration, you may receive personalized or non-personalized advertisements.
  • You can manage or reset your Android Advertising ID through your device's privacy or advertising settings. Deleting the Advertising ID may limit personalized advertising but does not necessarily stop advertisements.

Google Play Games

  • Google Play Games is used for leaderboard functionality if you choose to sign in.
  • Your high score is submitted to Google Play Games when you participate in leaderboards.

Third-Party Privacy Policies

  • Firebase Privacy Information
  • Google's Advertising Privacy Policy

Children's Privacy

Sand Fall is intended for players aged 13 and above and is not directed at children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

Data Security

  • Game saves and high scores are stored locally on your device.
  • Data transmitted by Google's services is handled according to their respective security and privacy practices.
  • Google's Mobile Ads SDK encrypts collected data in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
